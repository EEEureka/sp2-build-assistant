# Continuous surfaces and local delivery review

Read this before changing a hull/superstructure inclination, a shared shell boundary, a cut silhouette, or after a player reports repeated gaps/protrusions. Work through the whole affected boundary before another detail task.

Use [goal-driven building loop](build-loop.md) to maintain the original targets. After every coupled edit, separate subject findings from indirect-impact findings and use both to create the next iteration; reopen affected accepted targets. Missing or uncertain evidence creates a check target first. The manual helper below enforces only its declared finite plan; passing that list does not establish that the inventory of neighbors, interfaces and unknowns was complete.

## Plan the region, including its perimeter

Freeze a fresh snapshot and actual native mesh at one identity. List the editable semantic volumes, retired pieces, protected edits, all geometrically adjacent unchanged neighbors, and every perimeter interface. Native connection edges are not geometric adjacency. A local slope change affects floors, roofs, adjacent fore/aft sections, cutter transforms and the mirror side; inspect that closure before writing. Keep genuine planform changes and hard chines explicit. For a continuous face, share a plane/contour parameterization rather than fitting both sides independently.

Use unique artifact labels or directories for each exported state. Bind the mesh's own `identity` and `sampleId`, not a similarly named older result file. Compare the plan identity with the native export identity, and compare the final review identity with a fresh live read before delivery. The helper rejects a mismatched captured identity and non-world exports; it cannot know whether an internally consistent historical report is the current live state.

Changing the beam of a parallel body requires following the width envelope to **both the stem and the transom**, including the lower gray band, waterline, underwater skin, deck edge, roof and carved openings. A connector with coincident endpoints is insufficient if it introduces a sudden neck or shoulder. Record transverse widths and actual face normals at each segment boundary and independent stations between boundaries; retain the intended narrowing of the ends. Do not declare a region complete because a hand-written list of checks passed: first enumerate its geometric neighbors and reconcile that inventory against the check list. Rebind old merged parts by semantic ownership when doing before/after comparisons; numeric IDs alone are not a comparable regional scope.

Inspect source completeness and local oblique Blender views. A three-view silhouette does not describe recesses, thin returns, upper lips, or whether a transition is flat in the middle and curved only at its sides.

`regional.review` now enumerates the finite captured triangle mesh's boundary, crease, non-manifold and triangulation edges, coordinate extrema and normal-change vertices in `boundaryReview`. Automatic section stations include vertex events and independent intervals; excluded events remain explicit when budgets are exhausted. `impactPropagation` follows actual boundary-span candidates and declared attachment/cut/mirror/expected-interface relations through the union of before/after neighborhoods. Read its paths and pending items, not just the final affected-ID list. Enumerating topology does not prove that a boundary should be smooth or closed.

For a declared complete local interface, select its actual exterior with `expectedInterfaces` using `a`/`b`, separate `maxGapM`/`maxAngleDegrees`, and either a world `boundaryPlane:{axis,stationM}`, explicit `boundaryEdgeIdsA/B`, or `boundarySelection:"all_free_edges"`. Separate `boundaryPlaneA/B` and `normalHintA/B` allow different outer-face selections. The full-boundary check certifies both directions over the selected finite segment domains with adaptive distance bounds and all relevant incident normals. A points-only check remains finite sampled. Unknown face selection, missing source mesh, unmatched normal coverage or budget exhaustion cannot pass a complete goal. Use `crease:true` only with a justified `creaseReason`.

For a complete reference-domain comparison, provide explicit `referenceMatches:[{partIds,referencePartIds,goalId,maxSurfaceDistanceM}]` and a provenance-bound reference mesh with an explicit affine world transform. Both actual-to-reference and reference-to-actual distances are bounded over the selected triangle domains; both exports must declare completeness. Actual section overlays remain diagnostic between measured stations. Without a declared tolerance, the report requests review instead of inventing a pass. Local certificates do not become full-ship certification.

## Select the shape generator that owns the edge

Prefer an existing continuous hollow fuselage with controlled corners/edges and native planar cutting. Native Hole remains useful for openings and trimming. In the inspected 0.7.9 implementation, the trapezoid Hole calls `SimpleInset.Inflate` with a fixed 20 vertices per turn (about five vertices at a right-angle corner); its property schema has no sample control. Increasing the host's corner samples cannot refine that boolean edge. Recheck this implementation/schema after game updates.

In game **0.7.9.104**, Hole and Window can both have native type `ModifierWindow-1`; the explicit `ProceduralWindow` `hideGlass` state distinguishes the role. A Hole can have no visible triangles and still cut its targets. Discover `properties.describe/get` and edit the returned property IDs through `properties.set`; do not guess raw XML `height`, `depth` or target `partIds`. `MultipleParts` uses dynamic native intersections; `Custom` uses `customPartIds`. Re-read effective targets and both host/cutter geometry after a pose or outline edit.

The 0.7.9 observation is version-specific evidence, not a permanent Hole limit. Track game/plugin/capability versions and the exact edge owner. A host's outer rim, a Hole-generated inset, and a longitudinal Curved edge may use different generators and budgets. If the current version or native behavior is unknown, return a calibration/inspection task rather than assert that the shape must be split. Count actual visible segments and measure chord error after cutting.

For a concave quarter-frame, a trimmed hollow fuselage can supply its inner rounded boundary with native corner samples, while square Hole trims the horizontal limits. This is an alternative to compare, not a universal recipe: remove the other three returns, constrain both ends and validate actual inset/chord error. A circle fitted to the middle alone can leave an unwanted bottom rail or upper triangle. More samples repair tessellation, not a wrong cut outline. Prefer native serialized shape controls to runtime-only geometry that requires a plugin to display correctly.

Establish which native generator owns each face. A longitudinal hollow fuselage includes a full-beam top return; it is not merely two side walls or a narrow rim. That return can overlap an independent deck even when every outer-side distance check passes. Remove the redundant return using native cutting or a targeted Hole, and keep one visible owner for the deck surface. Extend boolean trims slightly past target end planes; an exactly coplanar cutter boundary can leave duplicate end-cap fragments. Verify the actual result rather than assuming subtraction at the boundary.

A raked stern has different longitudinal endpoints at different heights. Do not collapse every underwater band to the same distant zero-width section when the reference's complete U section ends earlier. Compare a continuous native U-shaped fuselage with a targeted planar Hole: a virtual loft section may exist outside the source only if the native trim removes that entire extrapolated region. Copy the actual neighbor's complete section controls and sampling at the shared end, rather than copying endpoint dimensions from retired segmented approximations. Validate the resulting outer skin, transom closure and material boundary. Cutting a hollow volume may expose its cavity; use a suitable closed native volume when the transom must be filled, check its mass/fuel/buoyancy effects, and do not conceal the cavity with paint or a cover plate.

## Required evidence before delivery

Use the existing `sp2model visual capture` with explicit `groups` of part IDs and `front`, `right`, `top`, `isometric` views to obtain labeled native-mesh cluster sheets. Group each cutter with its actual target host, and keep counterpart and boundary groups available. Open the recommended sheet and the individual group/detail files when labels or a cut contour are too small. These ID views help connect shape and ownership; combine them with `designer capture` for actual native appearance and occlusion. A functional hidden-glass Hole may have no visible mesh of its own: retain its ID/target relationship rather than inferring redundancy from the image.

For automated native-camera plans, check `contextMissing` and per-subject `contextCoverage` as well as individual images. A passed subset cannot certify a subject that never received a view. Feed SHA-bound `occluded` or `uncertain` results back into the next observation to change angle/zoom; preserve unresolved visibility instead of counting framing alone as acceptance.

- C0: sample both actual surfaces along every shared edge, including full height/length and perimeter neighbors. Reject missing faces; do not let a nearby end cap satisfy an outer-side probe.
- C1 where smooth: compare actual outer-face normals as well as position. A zero gap with different normals remains a visible fold. Give each preserved hard crease its physical reason.
- A tessellation vertex can have more than one incident face normal. Check every vertex for C0, and place C1 witnesses inside every incident outer edge/face to avoid comparing arbitrary nearest-triangle tie winners. Retain both reports; do not loosen a normal threshold merely because a vertex probe failed.
- At a sharp real chine, supply `normalHintA` and `normalHintB` for the two outer faces separately. A single common hint can reject the correct steep bow face and select a distant face instead. Inspect a failed probe before changing geometry or tolerances; keep end-cap rejection enabled.
- Both directions: source-to-candidate proximity cannot reject extra triangles. Inspect complete candidate contours and both outline extrema, plus source coverage. Check at endpoints, extrema, curvature changes and independent intermediate stations.
- Compare both **exposed trim contours** where two skins meet, not just a point where their planes intersect. Extending both skins can produce a valid intersection while leaving two exposed returns or a protruding lip. The helper's `trimProfiles` compares the upper skin's lowest exposed edge with the lower skin's highest exposed edge at independent longitudinal stations. Reject a double edge even if intersection probes pass. Fit shared native contours jointly; use Curved where a straight loft cannot express the lower curve, and split only at a documented contour break. Revalidate and retire the replaced primitive.
- Separate curve quality: measure chord error/segment turning angle at the visible edge. A 0.30 m shape budget does not authorize visibly coarse polygonal curves. Do not infer curve quality from triangle count alone.
- Openings/duplicates: inspect all loops, internal returns, coincident same-facing surfaces and the relevant opening/back-wall rays. An outer envelope alone cannot detect an interior hole or z-fighting.
- An overlap report must give the actual intersection polygon, area, plane normal and a witness inside that polygon. Averaging the two original triangle centroids is not an intersection witness. Classify visible coplanar skins separately from buried shared caps using geometry and opened views; an AABB overlap alone does not justify deleting a structural part.
- Read authored parameters **and** actual mesh after native rebuild. On observed cutter/host translation mismatch, use a guarded `craft.refresh`, then remeasure; do not loosen tolerances to accommodate a cache error.
- Preserve player edits field by field. If the requested continuity fix necessarily changes a player-adjusted deck's side edge, retain its pose, length, height and other intentional controls, and record the exact changed fields. Do not label that part wholly unchanged.

The standard-library helper [native_mesh_review.py](../scripts/native_mesh_review.py) reads only native mesh exports. Its JSON plan binds an identity and enumerates `requiredChecks`, `seams` (both surfaces and normals) and `silhouettes` (both extrema at each height). Missing declared coverage or a missing mesh is an error. It is a finite geometric check, not whole-craft certification. Keep failing reports and revise the construction, never overwrite a failure with a looser threshold without a documented reason.

## Make the defect large enough to see

Use native 2048-pixel close views and open every returned image. Include both sides, an oblique view, and a grazing view across the boundary. Orthographic framing requires explicit `orthographicSize`; `distance` alone does not set its scale. Verify the returned camera, target ROI and `cameraRestored`. If the target occupies only a small part of the image, recapture before judging it. More output pixels cannot rescue whole-ship framing for a centimetre-scale defect. Combine pixels with actual geometry rather than treating aliasing as proof of a gap or assuming an invisible gap is absent.

`parts.observe` provides actual-mesh PNG/SVG context, isolation, section, relationship and same-frame comparison images. PNG lines use subpixel coverage antialiasing; SVG supports lossless zoom. Neither substitutes for native paint, transparency or pixel visibility. Image budgets are shared across both local sides; inspect `skippedImages` and omitted-triangle limits. Before/after/reference layers retain distinct identities and explicit transforms; never align by visual guess alone.

`nativeViewPlan` supplies balanced opposite local directions, oblique and grazing camera requests with world witnesses, ROI, predicted feature pixels and ray-based occlusion. These are local surface directions, not inferred port/starboard labels. Set `captureNativeViews:true` for read-only native capture, then open its images. Reuse the saved same-identity snapshot/mesh and `nativeCaptureResults` with `nativeImageReviews:{VIEW_ID:{sha256,status:"inspected",visibility:"visible",outcome:"passed"}}` to record genuine inspection. A passing `nativeViewVerification` requires native file hashes/dimensions, current identity, healthy scene, restored camera, adequate projected pixels and visible inspected content. Occluded, uncertain or unseen images remain unaccepted.

Finish with a fresh diff, graph/scene health, native save and readback. State which interfaces and independent views passed, the retained real creases, player fields changed and unobserved regions. Do not claim the whole ship matches the reference from regional evidence.
